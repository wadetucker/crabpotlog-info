CrabPotLog Support

Privacy Policy

Developer: Wade Tucker
Effective and updated: 5 October 2026

CrabPotLog is a local-first crab-pot log for iPhone and Apple Watch. This policy explains information handled by the app and this website.

Your log stays on your devices

Pot coordinates, GPS accuracy, fix times, drop/check/pickup timestamps, names, catch counts, notes, record identifiers and source-device labels are saved locally. Optional catch photographs are processed into local JPEG copies on iPhone. CrabPotLog has no developer-run server, app account, Firebase integration, advertising, tracking or analytics.

Location, compass and photos

With your permission, foreground location marks pots and supports Find pot. Foreground compass readings help show direction; no compass history is recorded. There is no background location-tracking service. Camera use and selecting photos through Apple's picker are optional. The app does not scan or upload your photo library. You can change location, Precise Location and camera permissions in system Settings; this does not erase saved records.

iPhone and Apple Watch

Apple Watch Connectivity transfers log metadata between your paired devices, including coordinates, timestamps, catches, notes, identifiers, device labels and photo filenames. Delivery may wait for reconnection. Photo image files stay on iPhone. This is not a developer cloud-sync service.

Apple services and subscriptions

Apple provides location services, maps, satellite imagery, App Store purchases and billing. Map requests may involve Apple processing information under its policies. Map imagery is not guaranteed offline. Device backups and iCloud Photos depend on your Apple settings.

Apple handles payment information. CrabPotLog does not receive your payment-card details or Apple Account login credentials. On your device, StoreKit supplies verified acquisition and transaction information, product identifiers, expiry, renewal, revocation and family-sharing status to determine access. Receipts and purchase data are not sent to a developer server.

The initial-access period uses Apple's verified original acquisition date when available. A device-only Keychain marker retains the earliest known access date and latest observed time to discourage restarting the period by reinstalling or rolling the clock back. This marker may survive deleting the app; persistence and protection are best effort, not absolute. The app does not provide a control to reset that marker.

Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Manage subscriptions in your Apple Account settings. Cancellation does not erase Apple's purchase history. See Apple's privacy policy and subscription cancellation guidance.

Sharing and retention

CSV export and photo sharing occur when you choose them. Exports can include sensitive fishing coordinates, notes, timestamps, catches, identifiers and photo filenames; CSV does not include image files. Selected recipients or services handle these copies under their own policies. Images may reveal people or locations. Review private information before sharing; CrabPotLog cannot remove externally shared copies.

Logs and photographs remain in local app storage until that data is removed. Picking up a pot preserves history; note or name changes do not erase earlier events. The app currently has no individual record-deletion function. To remove local data, delete—not offload—the app on both devices. A remaining companion copy or restored backup may restore records. Manage backup copies and exported files separately. The Keychain trial marker may remain after uninstalling. No developer-held copy of your pot log exists for remote deletion.

Support and privacy questions

Contact Wade Tucker through CrabPotLog support Issues. Issues are public: do not post precise pot locations, private photographs, payment details or sensitive personal information. Start with a general question.

Support messages, GitHub usernames and attachments you submit are visible to the developer and GitHub to address your request. Public posts may remain available or be copied by others. Ask through the same channel about removing developer-controlled support content; GitHub controls its own platform records.

This website

This static site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It includes no tracking scripts, advertising, analytics, third-party fonts or site-added cookies. GitHub may log visitor information, including IP addresses, for service operation and security. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Children and changes

CrabPotLog is a general-purpose fishing log, not a service intended to collect children's information. Do not post children's personal information in public support requests. Parents or guardians can use the support link for questions.

If information handling changes, this policy will be updated with a new date. Apple, GitHub and sharing destinations have their own policies.
